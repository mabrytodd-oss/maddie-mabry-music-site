Book Me Live

Hey y'all! I'm a singer-songwriter from South Mississippi, and I first picked up a guitar when I was four and a half. Music has always been a steady thread through every season of my life— not just something I do, but part of who I am. Over the years, it's taken me from living room jam sessions to bigger stages, and I have had the same goal on each one: to give at least one person in the audience that joyous feeling that good music will leave with ya-- a feeling you just can't shake, but in the best way. For as long as I can remember, I've felt most like myself with a guitar in hand and a story to tell. I hope I get to tell you that story one day soon.

Glad you are here,

Maddie Mabry
